Phishing is a common online threat where attackers pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

STAY ALERT

5 Warning Signs of Phishing

Learn to recognize suspicious messages before taking action.

⚠️

Unexpected Urgency

Messages claiming your account will be blocked unless you act immediately may be suspicious.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links may lead to fake websites designed to collect your information.

🔐

Requests for Sensitive Data

Be cautious of messages asking for passwords, verification codes, or financial information.

🎭

Impersonation

Attackers may pretend to represent banks, delivery companies, or familiar services.

✉️

Unexpected Attachments

Unknown attachments can contain harmful files. Avoid opening unexpected files.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Example of a Suspicious Message

Imagine receiving a message that claims your bank account will be suspended unless you verify it immediately.

Subject: URGENT! Account Suspension Warning

Your account will be suspended today. Click the link below to verify your identity immediately.

PROTECT YOURSELF

What Should You Do?

  • Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
  • Check the sender and the actual website domain.
  • Never share passwords or one-time verification codes.
  • Visit official websites directly instead of using message links.
  • Report suspicious messages to the relevant organization.
