LINK SAFETY GUIDE

Think Before You Click

A link can look trustworthy while leading to a dangerous website. Learn how to inspect URLs before opening them.

Learn to Check Links

UNDERSTAND THE URL

What Should You Look For?

Pay attention to these common warning signs before clicking a link.

🔤

Misspelled Domains

Scammers may use domains that look similar to real websites but contain small spelling changes.

Example: paypa1.example instead of paypal.com

🌐

Unexpected Domain Names

Always identify the actual domain. A familiar brand name appearing somewhere in a URL does not prove the website is genuine.

Example: paypal.example.com

⚠️

Shortened Links

Shortened URLs hide their final destination. Be careful when you receive them from unknown people.

Tip: Verify the destination before visiting.

🔒

HTTPS Is Not a Guarantee

HTTPS encrypts the connection, but it does not prove that a website is honest or trustworthy.

Remember: A scam website can also use HTTPS.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Look Carefully at This URL

Example suspicious URL:

https://paypal.example.com/account-login

The actual domain here is example.com, not paypal.com. The word "paypal" is only a subdomain.

This example is for learning purposes. Do not enter personal information into unfamiliar websites.

STAY PROTECTED

Before You Click

  • Check the spelling of the domain carefully.
  • Look for unexpected subdomains and unusual addresses.
  • Be cautious with links sent by unknown people.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes through suspicious links.
  • When in doubt, visit the official website directly.
