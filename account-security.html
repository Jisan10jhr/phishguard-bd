ACCOUNT SECURITY GUIDE

Protect Your Digital Identity

Your online accounts contain valuable personal information. Learn practical ways to make them more secure.

Learn Account Security

SECURE YOUR PASSWORDS

Build Strong Password Habits

A strong password makes it harder for attackers to gain unauthorized access to your accounts.

🔐

Use Long Passwords

Choose long, unique passwords or passphrases. Avoid easily guessed information such as your name, birthday, or common words.

🚫

Never Reuse Passwords

Use a different password for each important account. If one account is compromised, reused passwords can put your other accounts at risk.

🗝️

Use a Password Manager

A reputable password manager can generate and securely store unique passwords, so you do not need to remember every one.

🔄

Change Compromised Passwords

If a password is exposed or you suspect someone has accessed your account, change it immediately and review your account security.

ADD AN EXTRA LAYER

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

🛡️

What Is MFA?

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) requires an additional verification method beyond your password.

Depending on the service, this may be an authenticator app, a security key, or another verification method.

Enable MFA wherever it is available, especially for email, financial, and important personal accounts.

WATCH FOR WARNING SIGNS

Could Your Account Be Compromised?

Pay attention to unusual activity such as:

  • Login notifications you do not recognize.
  • Password reset messages you did not request.
  • Unexpected changes to your account settings.
  • Messages sent from your account without your knowledge.
  • Unknown devices or sessions connected to your account.

TAKE ACTION

Protect Your Accounts Today

  • Use unique passwords for your important accounts.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where possible.
  • Review active sessions and remove unfamiliar devices.
  • Keep your devices, browsers, and apps updated.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes with anyone.
  • Use official websites and apps to access your accounts.
