ONLINE SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Safe in the Digital World

Your everyday online activities can expose you to scams, privacy risks, and cyber threats. Build safer digital habits to protect yourself.

Explore Safety Tips

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Online Threats

Understanding common threats is an important step toward protecting yourself online.

🎣

Phishing Scams

Fake emails, messages, or websites may trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other personal information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices, steal information, or give attackers unauthorized access. Avoid suspicious downloads and attachments.

🎭

Online Impersonation

Scammers may pretend to be trusted people, companies, or organizations to gain your trust and obtain sensitive information.

🔓

Privacy Risks

Oversharing personal information or using unsafe privacy settings can expose details about your identity and activities.

BUILD GOOD HABITS

Practice Safer Browsing

  • Check website addresses carefully before entering information.
  • Download software only from trusted sources.
  • Keep your browser, operating system, and apps updated.
  • Avoid clicking unexpected links in messages.
  • Use secure connections, especially on public networks.
  • Log out of accounts when using shared devices.
  • Think carefully before sharing personal information online.

PROTECT YOUR PRIVACY

Stay Safe on Social Media

👤

Review Privacy Settings

Check who can see your posts, profile details, and personal information.

🤝

Be Careful with Strangers

Do not automatically trust unfamiliar accounts, even when they appear professional or friendly.

📸

Think Before Sharing

Avoid posting sensitive information such as your address, financial details, or travel plans.

🚨

Report Suspicious Activity

Report fake profiles, scam messages, and suspicious content through the platform's official reporting tools.

RESPOND SMARTLY

If You Suspect a Scam

  • Stop interacting with the suspicious website or message.
  • Do not share passwords, verification codes, or financial details.
  • If you entered a password, change it using the official website.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Review account activity and contact the relevant service provider if needed.
  • Report the incident through an appropriate official channel.
