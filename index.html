STAY ALERT ONLINE

Think before you click.

Phishing messages often create urgency to make people act without checking carefully.

  • Check the actual domain name carefully.
  • Be cautious of unexpected password or payment requests.
  • Remember: HTTPS does not prove a website is trustworthy.
  • When unsure, visit the organization's official site directly.
